Our team and activities

Donate as little as Rs.50/month

About us:

We will go to places where there are needs.We only collect the smallest amount ever each month, just 50 rupees each person. With this money, we may aid the underprivileged by providing them with food and necessities like raincoats, blankets, and clothing.carrying out medical camps in indigenous areas.gaming equipment donation for orphan children.treating seasonal illness with medication.giving financial aid to those in need who are hospitalised.

Support for the journey:

We are taking in less money from each individual.  In the name of Aswathama, we manage a WhatsApp group. in that some people might be hesitant, we won't share every donation's specifics in this group. only admins can access this group, which is used for sharing. Only twice or three times every month will we share posts. We are raising this money to donate to orphans or individuals living on the side of the road.Every month, each group member continues to contribute.

Profitionalism and experience:

In the beginning stages of our trust, we are only managing 20 members.gradually grow the number of people in our WhatsApp group.In our WhatsApp group right now, there are about 500 Pluse members.As a result, we contribute more each month.We are providing 250 kg of rice and 200 kg of vegetables to support those who are struggling due to the flooding in Bhadrachalam. A medical camp is being held for rural communities.
